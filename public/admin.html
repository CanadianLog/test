How to put an Instagram Reel on the screen
  1. In the Instagram app, open your Reel or video post.
  2. Tap ⋯ (or the share ✈ button) → Download / Save to device. It goes to your photos.
  3. Open this dashboard on your phone, tap Choose pictures or videos above, and pick the video.

You can only download your own Reels. Videos with licensed music may download without sound.

Set up the Chromebook (one time)

  1. On the Chromebook, sign in and open the screen link above in Chrome.
  2. Install it as an app: click the install icon (⊕ / monitor with arrow) at the right of the address bar, or ⋮ menu → Cast, save, and share → Install page as app. It opens full screen with no browser bars.
  3. Make it start automatically: Settings → Apps → Restore apps on startup → choose Always restore. Leave the Ad Screen app open when you turn the Chromebook off (don't close it), and it reopens by itself after sign-in.
  4. Keep the screen on: Settings → Device → Power → “When idle”: Keep display on, and turn off “Sleep when cover is closed” if the lid is closed.
  5. Optional, so it boots without typing a password: Settings → Security and privacy → Lock screen → turn off “Show lock screen when waking from sleep”. For a fully hands-off kiosk see the README.

Ads are saved on the Chromebook, so they keep playing if the internet drops.
